## Priority matrix
P0: Protect successful hero/scroll; source-factual content; real form; accurate pricing; no fake portfolio; browser/build pass.
P1: Replace five blunt sections: current process, work, contact, why-us and service cards. Differentiate services and about internal pages.
P2: Rich route-specific 3D/media: targeted for actual usefulness, not everywhere. Optimize before adding new clips.

## Visual QA questions for EVERY route
1. Can a new visitor tell exactly what this page offers within 5 seconds?
2. Would screenshot alone show this is a DIFFERENT page rather than a recycled template?
3. Does the animated hero/object demonstrate a real workflow, product, feature or idea?
4. Is the page free of flat placeholder UI and repeated 'four generic cards' patterns?
5. Is the typography readable at 1440/1280/768/390 without awkward orphan words?
6. Can the user scroll forward/back without jank, and choose reduced motion?
7. Are actions live, keyboard navigable, responsive and useful?
8. Are all facts from brochure, explicitly proposed, or client-confirmation flagged?
9. Is every claim of case-study/result genuine and permissioned?
10. Does the price/scope/content match the buyer's likely decision at that route?

## Art direction scoring (internal, not claimed externally)
For each route score 1–5: Content usefulness; factual credibility; visual craftsmanship; differentiated layout; meaningful interactions; conversion; accessibility; performance. Any score <=2 requires repair before sign-off. No fabricated scoring—use actual screenshot reviews and observed behavior with written reasons.
