# Content decisions

## Factual (from the brochure, pending the owner's confirmation that it is current)
- Six solution families, 22 service lines, the solution-evolution spectrum, seven process stages, all 13 price bands (6 web, 3 software, 4 enterprise), software tier inclusions, enterprise capability matrix, price drivers and the "indicative starting ranges" disclaimer. See `content/FACT_REGISTER.md`.

## Proposed (editorial, ours)
- All headlines and explanatory copy (from the governance content blueprint, refined), studio principles on About, "typical deliverable" per stage, industry module examples, solution FAQs, scope questions.
- These are written as how the studio *proposes* to work ("we recommend", "typically"), never as audited practice or results.

## Illustrative (demos)
- Every interface preview, the hero panel thumbnails, the Concept Lab datasets, the industry workstation figures, the automation runner sample and the topology map. Each surface that shows them at readable size carries an "Illustrative" label.

## Withheld pending approval
- Contact details, legal name, address, team, founders, history, clients, results, testimonials, ratings: absent from the brochure, not published.
- Brochure "Why choose" claims "Expert Team" and "On-Time Delivery": unverifiable promises, not published.
- "Most popular" and "Domain included" badges (p.2): commercial claims, not published.
- Website-plan bullets that are illegible in the scan: withheld (counts kept in `src/content/pricing.ts`).
- Brochure logo vs site logo: the owner said the existing site logo is fine, so it is kept; logged for brand approval.

## Discrepancies flagged, not resolved
- Software tier C (₹5–15 lakh) overlaps enterprise tier 1 (₹10–15 lakh): explained on the pages as a difference in scope, not hidden.
- "Enterprise Web Solution" (₹80,000–₹1 lakh) vs enterprise platforms (from ₹10 lakh): explained in the enterprise FAQ.
- Brochure pagination shows "/12" but 8 pages were supplied; nothing is inferred about missing pages.
- Mobile apps and cloud/integrations have no standalone price in the brochure; their pages say so and point to the relevant tracks instead of inventing a band.
