## The idea
Home tells one story with one building: **the Omniwebora building, whose six arched bays are the six services.** The visitor watches an idea become that building and come alive, which is exactly what we do for a client's product. It follows the brochure's own words, **Innovate · Build · Transform**, and leads into the seven stages. The same building already carries the rest of the site (the six bays are the six rooms of the Solutions gallery; the terrace, the pavilion and the night scenes are all its grounds), so Home now introduces the world every other page lives in.

One fixed vantage on the terrace for the first four scenes (the subject transforms, like the Pricing model), then the camera walks in.
