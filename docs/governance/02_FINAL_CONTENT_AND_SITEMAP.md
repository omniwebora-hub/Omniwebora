## 0. Core brand narrative
**Category:** Digital solutions and software development partner.
**Promise:** Practical technology, tailored to how a business operates, built to scale with its needs.
**Voice:** Confident, clear, intelligent, measured, human. No 'revolutionary', 'world-leading', 'AI-powered everything', 'unlimited possibilities', unnecessary jargon or mysterious imagery.
**Primary audience:** Small/growing businesses seeking a website/e-commerce; operations managers needing custom workflows, ERP/CRM, dashboards; founders planning digital products; enterprise buyers needing integration, infrastructure and systems support.
**Primary conversion:** Project inquiry with requirements and preferred solution category.
**Secondary conversions:** Explore relevant service, view transparent indicative price band, explore actual demonstration, learn process, request a scoping call.
**Content convention:** Always distinguish proof vs example. The brochure advertises capabilities, but does not prove a particular deployed project.
