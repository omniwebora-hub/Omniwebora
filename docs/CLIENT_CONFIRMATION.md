# Omniwebora — items that need client confirmation before going live

- Official business name, domain, locations/address, support/contact email, phone, social accounts.
- Official logo source and permission; supplied screenshot is the only current branding reference.
- Service boundaries: deliverables, platforms, expertise, maintenance, AI capabilities.
- Portfolio case studies, screenshots, links, before/after results, client approvals.
- Approval for published figures: 20+ projects, 10+ industries, 100% client-focused; no public claims until verified.
- Actual company story, key people, team photos and testimonials with permissions.
- Preferred customer sectors/geographies, ideal deal sizes, project minimums and qualification criteria.
- Verified testimonials, logos, certifications, awards or partnerships (if any).
- Leads destination: real email/CRM, data retention policy, spam mitigation and privacy policy approved by legal/business owner.
- Production domain, hosting account, analytics consent, privacy and cookie setup, legal business information.
- Whether to display approximate budget ranges and response-time promises.
